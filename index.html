More from the Garden

Pollinator Patch

Bees visit our beds every morning and help the fruits and vegetables grow.

Details
We plant no-spray flowers along the edges so the bees always have food.

Pollinator Patch

Join our bee walk on the first Saturday of each month.

Root Vegetables

Carrots, beets and radishes grow in deep, loose soil that members prepare each spring.

Details
We sow carrots twice, so there is a summer harvest and a fall harvest.

Root Vegetables

Harvest days run on Sunday mornings from July to October.

Pepper Plots

Bell and hot peppers grow in our sunniest corner, next to the tomatoes.

Details
Peppers start green and turn yellow or red if you leave them on the plant longer.

Pepper Plots

Water peppers at the base in the morning and keep the soil mulched.

Strawberry Row

Our most popular row. Members pick berries here all through June.

Details
Pick only the fully red berries. The white ones need a few more sunny days.

Strawberry Row

Picking is open to members on weekday evenings from 5 to 8 pm.

Harvest Share

Extra produce goes into weekly boxes for neighbours and the local food bank.

Details
Volunteers pack the boxes every Friday with whatever is ready that week.

Harvest Share

Sign up on our contact page to get a box or to help pack them.

Flower Border

Bright daisies line every path in the garden.

Details
Members can take cut flowers home, as long as they leave some for the bees.

Flower Border

We plant new flowers each May. Message us if you want to help.
